# Privacy Policy, Gmail MCP

Last updated: 20 October 2026

## Who this applies to

Gmail MCP is a personal tool used only by its owner. No other person is invited to use it or to grant it access to their Google account.

## What data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests access to the owner's own Gmail account so that the owner can search, read, draft, send and organise his own emails from his AI assistant.

## How the data is used

- Emails are accessed only when the owner asks his AI assistant to perform a task on his own mailbox.
- The data is used only to carry out that task.
- The data is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics or the training of any model by the owner.

## Storage and security

- The OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local folder with restricted access.
- The app has no server and no database. Nothing is uploaded to any service operated by the owner.
- The tokens can be revoked at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

## Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, share or transfer Google user data to any third party. The only parties involved are Google (the Gmail API) and the AI assistant that the owner himself chooses to use on his own computer.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with an updated date.
